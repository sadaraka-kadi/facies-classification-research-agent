# Research notes: Well Log Facies Classification

## Data summary (free exploration of `data/train_data.csv`, before iteration 1)

Only the training file was explored. 3,232 rows, 8 wells, no missing values in any column.

**Class balance.** F2 22.8%, F3 19.0%, F8 15.4%, F6 14.3%, F1 8.0%, F5 6.7%, F4 5.7%, F9 5.0%, F7 3.0%.
Rare: F7 (98 rows), F9 (161), F4 (184), F5 (217).

**Per well, the class mix is very uneven.** This is the main risk for leave-one-well-out CV.
- F1 is concentrated in WELL_03 (158) and WELL_07 (89). WELL_04, 05 and 06 have none. Holding out
  WELL_03 removes 61% of F1 training rows, and it is the well with the most F1 to get right.
- F9 exists only in WELL_01 (50), WELL_02 (68), WELL_05 (12), WELL_06 (31). WELL_03, 04, 07, 09 have none,
  so any F9 prediction there is a pure false positive.
- F7 is 34 rows in WELL_01 and only 2 to 5 rows in WELL_03, 05, 09.

**Per-well log distributions.**
- Means: GR 60.7 to 72.7, PE 3.22 to 4.22, PHIND 11.6 to 15.7, DeltaPHI 1.6 to 7.7 (WELL_05 is the
  outlier at 7.71), ILD 0.57 to 0.76.
- Std: PHIND 4.8 to 10.6 and DeltaPHI 2.4 to 6.5 vary by more than a factor of 2 between wells, so
  absolute thresholds on these two will not transfer well. Part of this is real geology (each well's
  class mix differs), so per-well normalisation may also remove signal. That needs an experiment.
- Outliers: GR up to 361, PHIND up to 84.4.
- Mean Depth differs by well (2702 to 3019), so Depth encodes well identity. Do not use raw Depth as a feature.

**WELL_02 (synthetic, 68 rows, all F9).** Its logs have much higher PE (5.46 vs 3.2 to 4.2) and it
has a GR maximum of 222. Its depth is non-monotonic, which makes neighbour context there less meaningful.
(Corrected after iter 3: this line first said WELL_02 has the 3 repeated-depth rows. A check showed those
are in WELL_03 (2) and WELL_05 (1), and the GR 222 value was not verified to be a single sample.) It always stays in training and is 42% of all F9
rows, so it may push the F9 boundary towards its own PE range and increase false F9 in real wells
with high PE. It might also help recall. It stays a candidate for a sample-weight experiment.

**Vertical structure.**
- 84.4% of adjacent samples share a facies, pooled over the 7 real wells (range 0.80 to 0.90 by well).
  (Corrected after iter 3: this line first said 88%, which was a miscalculation.)
- Mean bed thickness is 4.9 to 9.4 samples (2.4 to 4.7 ft). Context windows should be small, about 1 to 3
  samples each side. Wider windows cross bed boundaries.
- Depth steps are 0.5 ft for 3,196 of 3,224 transitions, so a few gaps exist. Treating rows as an
  evenly spaced sequence is a good approximation, and I will sort by Depth inside each well.

**Structure that NM_M and Formation give for free.**
- NM_M splits classes almost cleanly: F1, F2, F3 are non-marine (99%, 1,590 of 1,612 rows) and
  F4 to F9 are marine (98%).
- Formation suffix agrees: `SH` is almost entirely F1 to F3 and `LM` is almost entirely F4 to F9.
  The Formation name is not in the baseline features and looks useful, particularly the
  distribution of F9 and F7 among `LM` members (B5 LM and B2 LM hold 126 of 161 F9 rows and 62 of 98 F7 rows).

**Which facies are physically similar (expected confusion).**
- Non-marine group: F2 and F3 (coarse and fine siltstone; 6 to 8% direct transitions per sample), and F1 and F2.
- Marine carbonate group: F6 and F8 (wackestone and packstone-grainstone; mean PE 4.23 vs 4.52, 8 to 9% transitions),
  F5 and F6, F4 and F6.
- F7 (dolomite) has the highest within-class GR spread with F4 (std about 49), so it will be unstable.
- Group means: F1 has low ILD (0.37) and low PE (2.91). F3 has highest PHIND (20.6). F4 has the highest GR (92).
  F9 has the highest PE (5.30).

**Modelling consequences to test.** Neighbour context within each well should help, since beds span several samples.
The NM_M split should be respected by any model. Most of the difficulty will be F2 vs F3, F6 vs F8, and
well-to-well shift.

## Stopping decision (after iteration 20)

Iterations 18, 19 and 20 are three consecutive iterations after iteration 14 with no improvement above both 0.01 and the measured seed spread over the best score, so the Part 2 stop rule applies. Step 17 (NM_M = 1 hybrid) was not run because splicing the archived confusion matrices of iters 8 and 9 estimated 0.5126, below the best.
